SevaRath Driver is the companion app for drivers of the Shantivan (Abu Road)
campus's EV ride service.

With SevaRath Driver you can:
• Go online or offline whenever you're available to drive
• Receive and accept or reject nearby ride requests
• Get turn-by-turn visual and voice navigation to pickup and destination
• Mark a ride as arrived, started, and completed as you drive it

SevaRath Driver is built for the Shantivan (Abu Road) campus community. There
is no fare, payment, or wallet feature — it is part of the campus's own EV
transport service. Driver accounts are set up by a campus administrator;
sign in with the mobile number and password they give you.
