SevaRath is the official ride-booking app for getting around the Shantivan
(Abu Road) campus by electric vehicle.

With SevaRath you can:
• Request an EV ride from your current location or a known campus location
• See your pickup point and destination on a live campus map
• Get matched automatically with the nearest available SevaRath EV and driver
• Track your driver's live location as they arrive and during the ride
• View your past rides in a simple ride history

SevaRath is built for the Shantivan (Abu Road) campus community. There is no
fare, payment, surge pricing, or wallet in the app — rides are provided as
part of the campus's own EV transport service, and a SevaRath account is
required to sign in.

Sign in with your mobile number or Google account to get started.
